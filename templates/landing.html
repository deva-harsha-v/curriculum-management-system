Enterprise Capabilities

Designed for Academic Excellence & Rigor

Streamlining departmental coordination, accreditation alignment, and curriculum lifecycle management across institutions.

Curriculum Lifecycle & Immutability

Formal version control supporting Draft, Published, and Archived stages. Once marked Published, curriculum schemes are locked from direct modification to preserve official academic standards.

Decentralized Change Requests

Faculty members propose structured course, syllabus, and credit revisions. Heads of Department inspect visual before/after diff tables and approve or reject modifications with complete atomicity.

OBE Correlation Matrices

Interactive correlation forms for Course Outcomes (CO) to Program Outcomes (PO/PSO), Department Mission to PEO, and PEO to Graduate Attributes with validated strength levels (1=Low, 2=Medium, 3=High).

Institutional PDF Generation

High-fidelity PDF curriculum compilation powered by ReportLab Platypus. Formats cover pages, semester course tables, syllabus outlines, outcome matrices, and prerequisite charts into publication-grade handbooks.

Department Isolation & Security

Robust data boundaries ensure HODs and faculty are strictly restricted to their designated department, preventing unauthorized cross-departmental edits while granting global administration to university leadership.

Comprehensive Audit Trail

Enterprise event logging recording every version creation, syllabus revision, change request approval, and document download with user identification, departmental scope, and exact timestamps.

Multi-Role Governance

Role-Based Access & Responsibilities

Each role is equipped with an intuitive workspace tailored to their exact institutional responsibilities.

SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR

Institutional Oversight

Manages institution-wide configuration, departmental directories, and user provisioning.

  • Manage academic departments & degree programs
  • Create and assign Head of Department (HOD) accounts
  • Activate, deactivate, and audit all system users
  • Create curriculum schemes across all departments
  • Global audit trail inspection and institutional oversight
HEAD OF DEPARTMENT

Departmental Governance

Controls department curriculum development, faculty onboarding, and academic validation.

  • Draft, update, and manage departmental curriculum versions
  • Onboard and assign faculty members to courses
  • Review faculty change requests with before/after diffs
  • Publish official curriculum versions and archive legacy schemes
  • Compile and download accredited institutional curriculum PDFs
FACULTY MEMBER

Curriculum & Syllabus Design

Designs course structures, syllabus units, and outcome mappings within department boundaries.

  • Formulate course syllabi, lecture hours, and credit structures
  • Define Course Outcomes (CO1 through CO5)
  • Map Course Outcomes to NBA Program Outcomes (POs/PSOs)
  • Submit structured Change Requests for HOD approval
  • Inspect and download published departmental regulations
